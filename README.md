## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, visitor guides, digital arrival declarations, and weather tracking.

- [Visit Singapore (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.visitsingapore.com/) - Official national tourist board guide featuring attraction highlights, cultural itineraries, and neighbourhood guides.
- [Singapore Arrival Card (SGAC) Portal](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard/) - Official Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) portal for mandatory pre-arrival electronic health declarations and entry submissions.
- [Singapore Tourism Board (STB)](https://www.stb.gov.sg/) - Lead government agency managing tourism development, industry licensing, and official sector data.
- [Meteorological Service Singapore (MSS)](https://www.weather.gov.sg/) - Official national weather agency providing real-time forecasts, regional thunderstorm tracking, and climate data.

## Transportation & Mass Rapid Transit

Navigating the Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) network, public bus systems, contactless payments, and aviation hubs.

- [Land Transport Authority (LTA / MyTransport.sg)](https://www.lta.gov.sg/) - Official government transport portal governing transit maps, road infrastructure, and public mobility standards.
- [Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)](https://www.changiairport.com/) - World-class international aviation hub featuring the Jewel complex, Skytrain transit, and direct MRT connections to the city center.
- [SBS Transit & SMRT Corporation](https://www.smrt.com.sg/) - Primary rail and bus operators managing comprehensive rapid transit lines across the island.

## Destinations & Regions

### Marina Bay & Downtown Core

- [Marina Bay Sands Official Guide](https://www.marinabaysands.com/) - Visitor portal for the iconic hotel towers, SkyPark observation deck, ArtScience Museum, and waterfront event spaces.
- [Gardens by the Bay](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/) - Official site for exploring the Supertree Grove, Flower Dome, Cloud Forest, and cooled conservatories.

### Orchard Road & Shopping District

- [Orchard Road Official Portal](https://www.orchardroad.org/) - Municipal visitor hub detailing premier retail malls, dining plazas, and street festival calendars.

### Sentosa Island & Resorts

- [Sentosa Development Corporation](https://www.sentosa.gov.sg/) - Official visitor portal for island beaches, resort hotels, theme parks, and cable car access.

### Heritage Districts (Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam)

- [Singapore Heritage Board (Roots.gov.sg)](https://www.roots.gov.sg/) - Official cultural heritage portal exploring the historic shophouses, temples, mosques, and walking trails of Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam.

## Nature & City in Nature

Resources for exploring Singapore's protected nature reserves, botanical gardens, and park connector networks.

- [NParks (National Parks Board)](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/) - Official government agency managing the Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO World Heritage Site), Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve, and island-wide park connectors.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contact numbers, medical assistance, and local safety rules.

- [Singapore Police Force (SPF)](https://www.spf.gov.sg/) - Official emergency and non-emergency police guidance (Dial **999** for police emergency assistance).
- [Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF)](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/) - Direct emergency dispatch for fire rescue and medical ambulance services (Dial **995**).
